# Freshness and Date Rules

Use these rules whenever a parent portal includes relative dates or old notices.

## Three dates, three meanings

- **Posted/upload time:** Determines whether a notice belongs in the requested reporting window.
- **Event date:** States when an activity, schedule change, or school event happens.
- **Deadline:** States when a parent must reply, submit, apply, or prepare something.

Never replace one date with another. A notice posted yesterday can contain a future event or deadline and should show both dates.

## Default reporting window

When the parent gives no period, include notices with an explicit posted/upload date of:

1. The current Asia/Seoul calendar date.
2. The immediately previous Asia/Seoul calendar date.

Do not include older notices simply because the body says “today” or because the notice still looks important. The parent may explicitly request history or a wider range.

## Relative language

Interpret “today”, “tomorrow”, weekdays, and similar language from the notice’s posting context. If the notice’s publication time is missing or unclear, do not infer freshness; mark it `확인 못 함`.

## Minimum evidence per action

Every reported action should retain:

- What the parent must do.
- When it is due or occurs, if stated.
- The notice title or a short identifying label.
- The explicit post/upload time when available.

Do not infer a requirement from a photo, decorative icon, or generic school practice when the source does not say it.
